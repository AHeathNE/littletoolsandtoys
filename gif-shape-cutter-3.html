GIF transparency is all-or-nothing (no soft edges), so the exported file will have a slightly harder edge than the live preview above. The export uses every real frame decoded from your GIF, so it matches the original animation exactly. Rendering happens entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Everything runs locally in your browser. Encoding uses gif.js, loaded from cdnjs when you export.
